About

I'm a computer engineer who kept getting handed the design work — and then went looking for more of it. Four years in, that's turned into a fest identity used across a campus, a daily social output at Feedspot, and a habit of building templates instead of one-off files.

The engineering half still shows up. I read the analytics on what I ship, test headline and thumbnail variants, and treat a post that didn't land as a brief rather than bad luck. Right now I split my week between SEO analysis and design at Feedspot in Borivali.

Graduated in computer engineering from Vidyavardhini's College of Engineering and Technology, Vasai, with an 8.13 CGPA.
